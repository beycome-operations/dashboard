Bonus rewards ticket volume, not same-day closing

KPI 2 pays on hitting a monthly ticket count, but the actual goal is closing same day. As written, an agent can miss every same-day close and still earn the KPI by working through a backlog. Worth resolving before the next bonus period.

Tickets and calls have split into two jobs

Kenneth is 52% of tickets but 25% of calls. Giebelle is 36% of calls but 22% of tickets. Nobody appears to have decided this, and it makes both of them hard to cover.

15h13m against 5h47m, while handling a fifth of Kenneth's tickets. He also has the best quality score on the team at 84.6%, so this may be thoroughness rather than a problem. Worth asking before treating it as one.
